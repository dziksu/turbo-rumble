Included unmodified GLBs: `bullet-foam-tip-thick.glb`, `grenade-b.glb`, `blaster-e.glb` and their external `Textures/colormap.png`.

Turbo Rumble scales and positions these models at runtime, adding its own geometry for rocket fins, warhead, mine casing, frost charge and mounts. The pulse coil and nitro tanks are procedural geometry. No remote asset requests are required by the game.
